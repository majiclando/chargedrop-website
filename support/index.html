ChargeDrop Support

ChargeDrop helps EV drivers compare reported charging prices, estimate session costs, save favorite chargers, and submit price updates before they plug in.

Contact

What to include

When contacting support, include:

  • Device model
  • iOS version
  • App version
  • Brief issue description
  • Screenshots, if helpful

Pricing disclaimer

ChargeDrop pricing is based on reported station data and may vary by station, charging network, time, fees, and availability. Always review the charger's posted price before starting a charging session.
