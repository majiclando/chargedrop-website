ChargeDrop Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

Overview

ChargeDrop helps drivers compare reported EV charging prices, estimate charging session costs, save favorite chargers, and report price updates. This policy explains the information the app may use to provide those features.

Accounts and guest browsing

You can browse charging information and save chargers on this device without an account. You need to sign in to submit price reports.

If you create an account, your email address and sign-in information are processed by our authentication service, Supabase.

Location

If you allow location access, ChargeDrop may use your device location to estimate nearby charger distance and drive time. You can manage location permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

Saved chargers and preferences

Saved chargers, estimate preferences, and related app preferences may be stored locally on your device.

Submitted price reports

If you submit a charging price report, ChargeDrop may store the station, price, report time, report source, confidence information, and app-generated report information needed to show updated pricing in the app.

Charging screenshots

When you ask ChargeDrop to read a screenshot, the selected image is sent to our backend for text extraction, which may use OpenAI. If you submit a report with a screenshot, it may also be stored privately as report evidence. Review screenshots for personal information before uploading.

Data sale

ChargeDrop does not sell your data.

Third-party services

ChargeDrop may use third-party services for app hosting, database storage, infrastructure, analytics, crash reporting, or related operational needs.
